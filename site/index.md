# Calendaric

**TL;DR:** Calendaric is an Obsidian plugin. It shows a month calendar in the sidebar and creates one note per day, week, month or year. It is for people who keep daily or weekly notes in Obsidian.

It replaces four older plugins in one: Calendar, Periodic Notes, the core Daily Notes plugin, and the library they shared. It works on desktop only.

## Install (version 0.1.0)

1. Download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from the [0.1.0 release](https://github.com/matisnape/obsidian-calendaric/releases/tag/0.1.0).
2. Put them in `<your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/obsidian-calendaric/`. The folder name must be exactly that.
3. In Obsidian, open **Settings → Community plugins** and switch on **Calendaric**.

The [user manual](guides/user-manual.html) has the full walkthrough.
